Master Your Brew

Extraction Protocols

Step-by-step masterclasses to unlock the optimal flavor profile from every bean.

1

Weigh & Grind

Measure 15g of coffee ground to a medium-fine texture (resembling table salt).

2

The Bloom

Pour 45g of 93°C water over the grounds and let degas for 45 seconds.

3

Steady Pouring

Pour in concentric circles up to 250g total water weight. Total draw down in 3 mins.

1

Coarse Grind

Use 30g of coarsely ground coffee for every 500g of hot water.

2

Steep 4 Minutes

Pour water, stir gently, and place the plunger on top without pressing down.

3

Press & Serve

Slowly push the plunger down and decant immediately to avoid over-extraction.

1

Dose & Tamp

Dose 18g into the portafilter basket, distribute evenly, and tamp level with 15kg pressure.

2

Extraction

Pull shot at 9 bar pressure aiming for 36g out in 27 to 30 seconds.
